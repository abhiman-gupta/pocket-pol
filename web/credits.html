Credits and licenses

Pocket Pol is an independent browser adaptation of polarized rendering from Mitsuba 3 and Pollia.

Mitsuba 3

Pocket Pol builds on Mitsuba 3’s Cornell box scene and ships Mitsuba’s license and preferred citation. Wenzel Jakob created Mitsuba at EPFL’s Realistic Graphics Lab. Its preferred software citation names Wenzel Jakob, Sébastien Speierer, Nicolas Roussel, Merlin Nimier-David, Delio Vicini, Tizian Zeltner, Baptiste Nicolet, Miguel Crespo, Vincent Leroy, and Ziyi Zhang. Thank you to everyone who has contributed to Mitsuba.

Pollia and PolRay

Pollia is developed by cmtribble3, abhiman-gupta, and kupinski on GitHub.

The interactive window follows Pollia’s Swift interface, and the JavaScript renderer adapts its Jarecki material implementation, polarized emitter, and Mueller transport. Pollia’s nylon material parameters and color tables come from PolRay.
