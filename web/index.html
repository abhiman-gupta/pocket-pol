Image Adjustments (Advanced)

Range
–

Maximum must be greater than minimum.

Unpolarized light
Light whose electric field has no preferred direction, like most lamps and sunlight. It is the default here: degree of polarization 0%.
Degree of polarization
The fraction of the light that is polarized, from 0% (unpolarized) to 100% (fully polarized).
Angle of polarization
The orientation of the polarization, from −90° to +90°, measured from horizontal.
Ellipticity
The shape of the polarization: 0 is linear, +1 right circular, −1 left circular, and values in between are elliptical.
Max path depth
The most surfaces a ray of light may bounce off on its way from the light source to the camera. Each bounce can change the light's polarization through the Mueller matrix of the surface it hits.
Low depth (1–2)
Depth 1 shows only the light source itself, and depth 2 adds direct lighting: surfaces lit straight from the lamp, with hard shadows and no color bleeding.
Higher depth (3 and up)
Adds indirect light that bounces between walls: softer shadows, red and green light spilling onto the white surfaces, and polarization patterns built up over several reflections. Renders take longer as depth grows, and the image changes less after a few bounces.
